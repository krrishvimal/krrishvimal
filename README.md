### 1. Spec-Driven Product Discovery (PRDs First)
Every feature starts with customer pain, not assumptions. I author structured Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) defining user personas, user stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and North Star metrics before engineering kicks off.

### 2. Rigorous Product & Funnel Analytics
I don't rely on gut feeling. I analyze end-to-end funnels—from acquisition to activation and conversion—using statistical rigor (Two-Proportion Z-tests, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR controls to eliminate peeking bias) and SQL queries to identify exactly where and why users drop off.

### 3. AI Product Architecture & Evaluation
As AI becomes the core interface of modern software, I design products with deterministic guardrails: multimodal vision evaluation, structured JSON schemas, latency vs. cost trade-offs, and zero-hallucination boundaries.

### 4. Technical Empathy & Unfair Prototyping Velocity
Having hands-on engineering depth in Next.js, Supabase, and PostgreSQL means I speak the exact language of engineering teams, accurately estimate sprint complexity, and can prototype functional POCs in 48 hours to test user demand.

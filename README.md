## 🚀 Featured Product & Analytics Prototypes

### [Funnel Doctor AI](https://funneldoctorai.vercel.app/)
> **Checkout diagnostics prototype for D2C storefronts** · [Live Prototype ↗](https://funneldoctorai.vercel.app/)

- **The Problem:** Direct-to-consumer brands often observe high drop-offs at checkout, but aggregate analytics fail to surface browser-level or visual friction points.
- **What I Built:** A diagnostic prototype where users submit a storefront checkout URL. The tool integrates **Google Gemini** to inspect mobile viewport screenshots and DOM structures for potential friction points—such as non-numeric keypads on phone inputs, unexpected layout shifts, or confusing payment options.
- **Statistical Evaluation:** Ran **1,000 simulated A/A tests** to verify how often the analysis flagged false positive differences between cohorts sharing identical conversion rates. It applies the **Benjamini–Hochberg procedure** to manage false discovery rates across multiple simultaneous checks.
- *Data Clarification:* Razorpay indicators reflect test-mode results, and dashboard metrics in the demo are illustrative simulations. *(Source repository remains private).*

## 🎯 How I Approach Product Roles

1. **Investigate Friction Before Adding Features:** Quantitative data shows *where* users leave; qualitative inspection and DOM reviews uncover *why*.
2. **Respect Statistical Baselines:** When evaluating user flows, I account for sample variation and multiple comparison corrections rather than claiming false-positive "wins."
3. **Prototype to Validate Hypotheses:** Rather than relying solely on slide decks, I build functional prototypes to put working interfaces in front of real users early.
